Our Story & Mission

Redefining Food Delivery with 20-Minute Flash Speed.

CraveRush was founded with a singular promise: to connect food lovers with their favorite restaurant meals piping hot, impossibly fresh, and faster than anyone else.

It started in 2021 with three friends, one scooter, and a shared frustration: great restaurant food almost always arrived cold, soggy, or hours late. So we rebuilt delivery from the ground up — our own dispatch engine, our own insulated packaging, and a rider network trained to treat every bag like it's their own dinner. Today CraveRush operates in dozens of neighborhoods, but the promise hasn't changed: real food, from real kitchens, at real speed.

We're not just a delivery app. We hand-pick every restaurant partner, audit their kitchens monthly, and give riders smart routing so your order takes the fastest possible path from stove to doorstep. Whether it's a 2am biryani craving or a Sunday morning coffee run, CraveRush exists to make "hungry" a very short-lived problem.
