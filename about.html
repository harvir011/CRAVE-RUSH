How It Started

Three friends, one scooter, and a very impatient appetite.

It started in 2021 with a shared frustration: great restaurant food almost always arrived cold, soggy, or hours late. So we rebuilt delivery from the ground up — our own dispatch engine, our own insulated packaging, and a rider network trained to treat every bag like it's their own dinner.

Today CraveRush operates across dozens of neighborhoods and works with over a thousand kitchen partners, but the promise hasn't changed: real food, from real kitchens, at real speed. We hand-pick every restaurant partner, audit their kitchens monthly, and give riders smart routing so your order takes the fastest possible path from stove to doorstep.

Whether it's a 2am biryani craving or a Sunday morning coffee run, CraveRush exists to make "hungry" a very short-lived problem.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make restaurant-quality food accessible within 20 minutes of ordering, anywhere in the city — without compromising on temperature, freshness, or the dignity of the riders and kitchens that make it possible.

Our Vision

A future where "delivery food" and "fresh food" mean the same thing — where every neighborhood has instant access to the city's best kitchens, powered by a fully electric, zero-emission fleet.

Milestones

From one scooter to a citywide network.

2021

CraveRush is founded

Three friends launch CraveRush in Chandigarh with a single scooter, a handful of restaurant partners, and a promise: 20 minutes or your money back.

2022

Expansion to 5 cities

Smart dispatch routing goes live, cutting average delivery time from 34 minutes to under 20 across every launch city.

2023

100% electric fleet

CraveRush retires its last combustion-engine scooter, becoming one of the first fully electric food delivery fleets in the region.

2024

1,000+ kitchen partners

Monthly hygiene and quality audits become mandatory for every partner restaurant, crossing the 1,000-partner mark.

2025

CravePass membership launches

Free delivery, exclusive discounts, and priority dispatch roll out to CraveRush's most loyal customers.

2026

100,000+ happy customers

CraveRush crosses 100k satisfied customers and a 4.9★ average rating, with the 20-minute guarantee still going strong.
